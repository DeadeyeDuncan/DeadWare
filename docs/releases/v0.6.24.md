# DeadWare 0.6.24 — Connections map details

- Replace the all-connections table and page controls with a compact list of the nodes currently visible on the map. The list follows globe rotation, zoom, filters, and Live or History mode.
- Click a map node or its list entry to open a detail box beside it with the application, endpoint, approximate location, rates, transfer totals, observation times and coverage. The box follows the node as the map moves; use Close or Escape to dismiss it.
- Show saved connection details directly from historical map nodes, including observations beyond the former table's first page. Clear outdated details when switching modes, filters or history minutes.
- Keep grouped-location totals distinct from the representative connection's own traffic. Local and unlocated peers remain included in summary totals and saved-history CSV exports.
