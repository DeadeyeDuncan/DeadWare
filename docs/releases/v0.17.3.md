- **Controls and export status together.** Usage history's range, navigation and export controls share a card, with export results shown directly below them.
- **Three chart columns on wide pages.** At page widths of 1,100 DIP or more, the six Device charts use three columns; below that width, they use two. A shorter caption keeps mouse and keyboard guidance visible, with the full explanation in its tooltip.
- **Applications and Application resources tabs.** One lower card opens on Applications; activating a CPU, RAM or GPU point opens Application resources, and activating a traffic-strip slot opens Applications.
- **Compact recording settings.** Keep a longer record keeps recording and storage status visible, with retention, privacy and Clear history… inside the initially collapsed Retention, privacy and clearing expander.

This release changes the Usage history layout in the main page and separate window; recording, history, exports and keyboard controls are unchanged.
