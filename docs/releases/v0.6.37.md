- Cooling Health now shows your CPU cooler's pump speed. Choose your CPU and the **Fan speed** metric to see it, just as GPU fan speed is shown for graphics cards.
- DeadWare reads a motherboard or AIO header labeled as a pump (for example `Pump Fan` or `AIO Pump`), otherwise the `CPU Fan` header. Unlabeled headers such as `Fan #2` are not used.
- Turn on **Settings → Extended sensors** and restart DeadWare to read these headers. Systems with more than one CPU are not supported.
- Because the CPU's sensor mapping changes, CPU Cooling Health history and references start fresh under a new setup. Save a new CPU reference after at least 30 eligible minutes of normal use. GPU history is unchanged.

Existing updater-enabled installations can choose **Updates → Check for updates → Update & restart**.
