- **More room for the Connections map.** The Connections map card has a 420 DIP minimum height. Globe / 2D, zoom, Reset and Animate now sit in a glass toolbar at the map's top-right corner.
- **Visible nodes beside the map.** At page widths of 1,100 DIP or more, the visible-node list, Export CSV… and selection details sit in a 280 DIP column beside the map. Below that width, they move under the map, above the tabs.
- **Traffic and Destinations tabs.** One lower card groups the application traffic strip and the Destinations table. Live shows Destinations; entering History selects Traffic, with Destinations one tab away.
- **A slimmer controls card.** Mode, range and History interval/cursor controls share the first row; application/IP, country and direction filters share the second. Summary and capture status now appear inside the same card.
- **Folded database and recording controls.** Expand **Location database, recording and storage** for location-database downloads, recording, Home city and storage controls. Action and warning messages remain visible above it.

This release changes the Connections layout; data, queries, history, exports and recording behavior are unchanged.
