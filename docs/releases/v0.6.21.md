# DeadWare 0.6.21 — Connections map and history

- Open **Connections** to explore live application traffic on a rotatable globe or 2D map, with approximate country/city information, directional activity and a searchable application/peer table.
- Record optional device-local endpoint history, enabled by default, with pause/resume, up to 30 days of retention, filters, minute playback and CSV export. Saved locations reflect what was known when traffic was recorded.
- Enable **Connections map (live)** under **Settings → Widget graphs** for a compact 2D map, alone or alongside other graphs. It stays live while the main Connections page displays or plays history.
- Download an optional local **DB-IP City Lite** location database through **Download / update locations** and choose an approximate home city from the offline list. Observed peer IPs are never sent to an online location service.
- Keep local and unknown peers visible, show incomplete capture and recording gaps, and scale map markers and paths by observed traffic. Geographic paths are illustrative links, not measured routes or safety ratings.
