# DeadWare v0.4.1 — Longer live graphs

Choose **1 h**, **3 h**, or **6 h** above the dashboard or from a graph's **⋯** menu. The existing 1-, 5- and 15-minute choices remain available. All live graphs share the saved range selection, including both upload and download on the network graph.

Live history builds while DeadWare is running, up to six hours, and starts fresh after a full quit or restart. Cooling Health continues to provide its separate saved thermal history.

Longer graphs preserve peaks and missing-data gaps while reducing the drawing work to fit the chart width. The compact menu keeps its narrow size with two rows of range choices. Hidden dashboards and closed graph menus skip unnecessary statistics work.

Portable Windows x64 preview. Download the ZIP, extract it, and run `DeadWare.exe`. Fully quit an existing installation from the tray before replacing its files. Administrator mode is needed for some temperatures and per-app network accounting; sensor availability depends on hardware and drivers.
