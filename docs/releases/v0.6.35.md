- Open **Color theme**, then hover over **Elder Scrolls Themes** to choose **Skyrim** or **Mage**. The menu also supports keyboard navigation and marks your current choice.
- **Skyrim** pairs charcoal stone with silver text and frost-blue accents. **Mage** adds deep violet, arcane cyan and antique-gold accents.
- Both themes feature readable Nordic-style lettering, an original subtle stone texture, and decorative runes along live graph traces on the dashboard, detached graphs and desktop widget. Numeric readings remain clear, and missing readings remain gaps.
- Keep your custom graph colors and styles. Windows High Contrast uses system fonts and undecorated traces; disabled transparency keeps solid backgrounds. The selected theme is remembered across launches.

Existing updater-enabled installations can choose **Updates → Check for updates → Update & restart**. After restarting, open **Color theme → Elder Scrolls Themes**. Settings and recorded history are preserved.
