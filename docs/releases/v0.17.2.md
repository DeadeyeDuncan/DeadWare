- **More room for the drive chart.** The Storage detail card grows with the window and has a 360 DIP minimum height, giving the selected drive's read/write chart the available space.
- **Drives beside the chart.** At page widths of 1,100 DIP or more, drive selectors sit in a 280 DIP column beside the chart and scroll within the card. Below that width, they move above the detail card.
- **Volumes and Health tabs.** One lower card opens on Volumes for mounted volumes and free space; choose Health for power-on hours, highest temperature, remaining life and identity notes.
- **Status shown once.** Storage warnings stay at the top of the page; sensor-unavailable notes already shown there are no longer repeated in Health.

This release changes the Storage layout; drive selection, monitoring, history and pop-out behavior are unchanged.
