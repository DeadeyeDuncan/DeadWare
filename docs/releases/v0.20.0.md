## 0.20.0 — Tune each light device, and give keyboards their own modes

- **Brightness and colour correction for each device.** A device's card on the Lighting page now has **Brightness** (**This device**, 0 to 100%) and **Colour correction** (**Red**, **Green** and **Blue**, 0 to 200, where 100 leaves a colour as it is), with **Reset** for the correction. Use them when one device looks brighter than the others, or its white looks tinted. A device's brightness works with the page's **Brightness**: the two are multiplied, and the card says so while either is below 100%. At the starting values nothing changes from 0.19.0. A device running an effect of its own, such as the Lighting Node Pro, shows these controls greyed out, with a note, and keeps what you set.
- **Lighting modes for keyboards.** A keyboard's card has **Lighting mode**: **Follow the desk effect** (the default), **Static**, **Breathing**, **Colour cycle** and **Rainbow wave**, with **Speed** for the last three. A mode belongs to that keyboard and runs whatever the desk's effect is. **Rainbow wave** needs the keyboard to report where its keys are, and is greyed out, with the reason, when it doesn't. A saved mode a device can't run is shown on its card with the reason, and the device is lit as **Static** meanwhile; for a device that isn't a keyboard, **Follow the desk effect** clears it.
- **The keyboard, key by key.** On a PC with the desk picture, a keyboard that reports its key positions is drawn with every key in the colour it has now. The picture shows the colours as intended, before brightness and colour correction.
- **Going back to 0.19.0.** If you go back to 0.19.0 after tuning a device, 0.19.0 runs the lights but won't save lighting changes; your tuning is there again when you update.
