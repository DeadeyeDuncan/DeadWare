- DeadWare windows can now be see-through glass. Open **Settings → Window glass** and choose **Subtle**, **Balanced** (the default) or **Strong** to let the blurred desktop and windows behind show through the dashboard, graph pop-outs, battery history and widget. Choose **Off** for solid windows.
- Panels and controls thin out with the selected level, keep palette tints for readability, and gain brighter highlighted edges. The glass continues through the title bar.
- Windows shows the acrylic glass only in the focused window; unfocused DeadWare windows switch to the theme's solid surface until you select them again. Pop-up menus stay solid.
- Requires Windows 11 22H2 or later with Windows **Transparency effects** on. Blackout and High Contrast always stay solid.

Existing updater-enabled installations can choose **Updates → Check for updates → Update & restart**. After restarting, open **Settings → Window glass** to pick a level.
