# DeadWare 0.6.22 — Map-only Connections widget

- Show only the map in the Connections widget, without headings, legends, status text, speed readouts or hover tooltips.
- Fit the full world across the widget panel and fill its available height when no other graphs are selected.
- Place the map below all selected graphs, with scrolling that keeps both the graphs and map reachable on small displays.
- Keep the full Connections page's labels and controls, and preserve the widget's independent live data, themes and traffic colors.
