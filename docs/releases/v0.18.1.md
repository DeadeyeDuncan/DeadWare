## 0.18.1 — Widget size, saved graphs and map cost

- **The widget and pop-out graphs keep their size on a second monitor.** With monitors at different scales (for example a 150% main screen and a 100% second screen), a widget or pop-out saved on the other screen reopened smaller each time DeadWare started: two thirds of its size from 150% to 100%, and that smaller size was then saved. Windows rescales a new window as it is moved onto a screen with another scale; DeadWare now applies the saved size again once the window is there. A window that 0.18.0 already shrank stays as it is until you resize it.
- **Saved graph history no longer stops when its file is full.** The six-hour graph history is kept in a file limited to 64 MiB. At 0.5-second polling with several drives and fans, six hours do not fit (one PC filled it in under five), and once full DeadWare showed "Graph history unavailable (SqliteException)" and saved nothing more until old readings aged out and it was restarted. It now drops the oldest saved readings to make room, and the status reads "Graph history saved locally · the newest hours that fit its 64 MiB file". The graphs on screen were never affected while DeadWare stayed open; what was lost was the history shown after a restart. This also affected 0.17 and earlier.
- **The Connections map costs much less to animate.** While any connection was active the map redrew the oceans, land, coasts and borders from their outlines up to 30 times a second. They are now drawn once and reused until the view changes. Measured on one PC with only the widget and its live map showing, DeadWare's processor time fell from 27 to 18.5 seconds per minute; with the Connections page open as well, from 37 to 23.
