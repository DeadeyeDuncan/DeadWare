- The **Network** graph shows the local-network share of traffic. LAN, loopback and link-local peers from the per-app capture are drawn as muted traces under the download and upload totals, so the gap between them is the internet share. The widget and compact headers add `· local ↓ x ↑ y`, and the hover chip adds a **Local** line. When per-app capture is unavailable the graph is unchanged.
- When per-core CPU usage is shown, the aggregate CPU line is no longer drawn over the cores. The GPU comparison trace, the header percentage and the hover total remain.
