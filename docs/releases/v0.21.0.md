## 0.21.0 — Paint single keys, add layers, and save looks for keyboards

- **Paint keys.** On a PC with the desk picture, a keyboard's card has **Key colours** with a **Paint keys…** button. It opens a large drawing of the keyboard: click or drag across keys to pick them, use **All**, **WASD**, **Arrows**, **Number row**, **F keys** or **Number pad**, then choose a colour. **Remove paint** takes it off the picked keys or every key, and **Undo** steps back through colour changes until you press **Done**. A key you haven't painted shows the keyboard's lighting mode. The buttons need a keyboard that reports its key positions and its keys' names, and are greyed out on the plain desk.
- **Layers.** **Edit layers…** adds up to 8 layers on top of the keys' colours: **A colour**, **An effect** (Wave, Breathing or Spectrum), **Key fade** and **Key ripple**. Each has a **Strength**, mixes by **Covers it** or **Adds light to it**, and covers **All keys** or **Only the keys I pick**.
- **Key fade and key ripple notice key presses.** The lighting helper reads only which key went down or up, never what you type, and ignores keys that software sent. It remembers which lights were just pressed for up to three seconds and nothing longer, stores none of it and sends it nowhere outside DeadWare, and watches only while a layer needs it. While the Lighting page is open, the keyboard drawn there lights up with your presses too. A press on any keyboard on the PC lights this one, and reactions pause while an administrator window is in front unless DeadWare runs as administrator too.
- **Saved looks.** **Saved looks** on a keyboard's card keeps up to 12 named looks, each a copy of the painted keys, the lighting mode with its Speed, and the layers. **Save as a new look…**, **Save changes**, **Rename** and **Delete** manage them, and the drop-down says **(changed)** when the keyboard has moved on from the look. Brightness, colour correction and the Colour section are not part of a look.
- **Nothing changes until you use it.** With nothing painted, no layers and no looks, the colours and the saved file are exactly as in 0.20.0.
- **Going back to 0.20.0.** If you go back after painting keys, adding layers or saving looks, 0.20.0 runs the lights but won't save lighting changes; what you set is there again when you update.
