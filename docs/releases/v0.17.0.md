- **First network use alerts.** DeadWare adds one **first network use** rule, switched on, once: not when 64 rules already exist, and a rule you remove stays removed. It needs per-app capture, which runs while DeadWare runs as administrator, and learns quietly for 3 calendar days after capture first runs (that day and the next two, ending at local midnight); every app already in Usage history or Connections history counts as known. After that, the first time an app DeadWare does not know sends or receives traffic, one episode, "<app> used the network for the first time", names it. Apps seen within 60 seconds of the first share one tray balloon, shown when that minute ends: at most one balloon a minute, and not limited to one an hour like other rules. Each app alerts once. **Don't alert for this app** on its log row puts it on the Don't alert list (up to 256 apps), which keeps it quiet if it ever counts as new again; **Alert again** under the rules takes it off. **View in Connections** on a first-use row opens Connections History from 5 minutes before to 10 minutes after that minute, showing only that app, while Connections still keeps the minute (30 days). A line under the rule says whether it is waiting for per-app capture, learning, watching or not watching, with the number of apps known. Apps are told apart by executable name only, loopback traffic is not network use, and processes DeadWare names only by PID are never learned. alerts.db keeps the known apps' upper-case names with the first and last minute it saw each, until 400 days after the last; at 8,192 known apps the one seen least recently is forgotten.
- **Don't record these apps.** Settings → History and alerts → **Don't record these apps** lists up to 64 apps by executable name, such as chrome.exe. They still show on live pages (Processes, Network, Connections Live, the Destinations table and the widget) marked " · not recorded", but none of their history is saved from then on: no Connections history, no per-app totals in Usage history and no alerts for them, so application traffic budgets for them stop counting and first network use never learns or alerts them. In Application resources their CPU, memory, I/O and GPU count under Everything else, so device totals stay true. History saved before an app is added is kept until it is cleared. Processes that Windows saves as "PID n" can't be matched. On Connections History, an application filter for a listed app reads **Application: … · not recorded ✕**, and its recorded slots without saved traffic are hatched as not saved, never drawn as zero.
- **Clear history…**, in Settings → History and alerts, under Usage history's **Keep a longer record** and under Connections' **Recording, origin and storage**, removes saved history for one app or all apps, older than 7 days (the default) or from all time, after a confirmation. It clears the chosen apps' records from Connections history, Usage history's per-app totals, Application resources (one app's numbers move into Everything else; all apps removes those minutes) and the alert log's application budget and first-use alerts. It keeps device-wide history (CPU, GPU, memory, temperatures, network totals, battery and Cooling Health), other alerts, your alert rules and the Don't record and Don't alert lists. Rows saved as "PID n" or "Unknown application" are cleared only with All apps. An All time clear can drop up to 60 seconds of new history for the chosen apps. Clearing an app for all time makes its next network use alert again; clearing older than 7 days keeps it known. Connections history hides the cleared history at once; the other histories can show partly cleared data until they finish, with "Clearing history" in their status lines. Quit, sign-out, Restart as administrator, an update install or a crash interrupts a clear, and it resumes when DeadWare next starts ("Finishing a clear that was interrupted…"); a history whose clear could not start reports "not cleared". Freed space is reused for new history and the files don't shrink; exported CSV files and Windows notification history are not cleared. On both traffic strips, time cleared for all apps shows as a **Cleared** band with "Cleared before …", and recorded slots whose traffic a clear removed are hatched as Not saved.
- **Returning to 0.16 or older.** That build's first save drops the first network use rule and the Don't alert list from settings.json, because older builds drop unknown rule kinds and save at every launch. alerts.db keeps the known apps and the learning date, and older builds show first-use episodes with "peak 0". Reinstalling 0.17 adds the rule again once, even if it had been removed, with no new learning period; apps first used meanwhile alert at their next use, and apps that were on the Don't alert list are no longer on it. Going back to 0.16 or earlier drops the Don't record these apps and Don't alert lists from settings.json on that version's first save, so those apps are recorded again. A clear interrupted by Quit or a crash finishes when 0.17.0 or later next runs. An earlier version leaves the rest of it in place, and shows any rows of a one-app clear that are not yet deleted. Connections history that a clear already hid for all apps stays hidden, and that version purges it in one long maintenance pass at its next save. On 0.16, the Connections strip shows time cleared for all apps as 'Not kept before …', and both strips show other cleared minutes as if the cleared apps had no traffic then.
