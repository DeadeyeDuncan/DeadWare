Pop-out graphs now use a compact layout that gives more room to the graph and current reading.

- Resize graph windows smaller for stacking beside your work.
- Open the graph options menu for history range, Always on top, hardware information and minimum/average/maximum readings.
- The network graph keeps download and upload visible together.
- Existing saved positions, sizes and topmost choices are preserved. Drag a window edge to make it smaller; your new size is remembered.

Download the Windows x64 ZIP, extract the entire folder, and run DeadWare.exe. Close the app before replacing files. Keep the executable at the same location to preserve your startup registration and shortcut.

This remains an unsigned portable preview. Hardware support and administrator requirements are unchanged.
