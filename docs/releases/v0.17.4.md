- **Controls and operation status together.** Cooling Health's device selector, Refresh and Export CSV… controls share a card, with operation results shown directly below them. Range and metric controls sit beside Thermal history.
- **More room for thermal history.** The chart grows with the page's available height. At page widths of 1,100 DIP or more, Cooling assessment sits beside it; below that width, the assessment appears above the chart.
- **Reference, Warnings and Recording & setup tabs.** One lower card opens on Reference, with warnings and recording settings in their own tabs. The Warnings tab shows how many warnings need review even before it is opened.
- **Recording failures stay visible.** A history recording or read failure shows in the controls card as well as under Recording & setup, whichever tab is open.
- **Compact reference editor.** Save a new reference starts collapsed; open it when ready to save a reference period.

This release changes the Cooling Health layout; recording, reference comparisons, warnings and CSV exports are unchanged.
