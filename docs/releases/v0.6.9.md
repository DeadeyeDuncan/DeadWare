# DeadWare 0.6.9 — Adjustable data polling

In **Settings → Data polling interval**, choose **0.5, 1, 2, 5 or 10 seconds**. One second remains the default. Your choice is saved and takes effect after the current wait or reading finishes, without restarting.

Faster polling uses more resources. Hardware scans remain sequential and always leave an idle pause; slow hardware may take longer than the selected interval. The dashboard, graphs and widget share the same collector. Display freshness and history gaps follow each sample's interval, and graph history keeps its six-hour range at 0.5 seconds.

Cooling Health keeps its existing comparison quality requirements. At 5 or 10 seconds it records history but does not collect enough samples per minute for reference comparisons. Use a faster setting when collecting a reference or comparing cooling behavior.

Windows 10/11 x64 portable build, with the runtime and update helper included; unsigned.
