The line reads Turn lights off while the lights are on and Turn lights on while they are off. It is the same switch as on the page and the shortcut.

If lighting is switched off in Settings, or the lighting helper is not running, the line is greyed out and reads Lights (not available).

3 · Sleep and wake (nothing to look at)

When the PC goes to sleep: DeadWare stops sending anything to the lights.
When it wakes: DeadWare waits about 10 seconds for the devices to come back, looks for them again, and repaints everything the way it was. If the lights were off (switch, idle or lock), they stay off.
The page would show "Waking up… lights return in a few seconds" during those 10 seconds.

Choices I made for you (say a number to change one)

  1. Everyday card sits under the desk picture, full width, on the Lighting page. The other place would be Settings → Lighting; I chose the page so the shortcut is easy to find.
  2. The shortcut turns lights off and on (not only off), and you change it by clicking Change and pressing the new keys. "No shortcut" removes it. If another program already owns the keys, a red line says so.
  3. Idle time is a short list (1, 2, 5, 10, 15, 30, 60 minutes) rather than a number box, and starts at 10 when you switch it on. Idle and lock both start switched off.
  4. While idle or lock has the lights off, the Lights on switch stays on and a yellow line on the page says why the lights are dark. Idle and lock never change your saved choice.
  5. Lights cut off and on at once, no fade (as DeadLight).
  6. One mouse card for cable and wireless, one set of colours and settings for both.
  7. Battery shows a bar, a percentage and "Charging" / "On battery". Wireless, it is read only while you use the mouse (reading it would otherwise keep the mouse awake). No low-battery warning in this step.
  8. Mouse sleep time applies as soon as you pick it (no Apply button). It starts on "Leave as the mouse is", so DeadWare changes nothing on the mouse until you choose.
  9. Turning lights off also darkens a sleeping-soon mouse straight away. DeadLight left it lit until you moved it.
  10. Tray line says what clicking it will do ("Turn lights off" / "Turn lights on") rather than a tick mark.
